<div style="height: 20px;"></div>

<p style="text-align: justify">I am a Research Associate and PhD candidate at the Otto Suhr Institute for Political Science of <a href="https://www.polsoz.fu-berlin.de/en/polwiss/index.html"> Freie Universität Berlin</a>. I study the domestic and international <i>politics of decarbonization</i>. My current research focuses on the political conditions and consequences of institutional change and backlash in climate policy. I am also interested in broader questions around <i>public policy</i> and <i>political economy</i>. My research is mostly problem-driven, which means that I apply a wide range of theories and methods. While most of my research focuses on the German and European context, I have also studied climate and industrial policy in China, Brazil, and the United States. </p>


<div style="height: 10px;"></div>

<p style="text-align: justify">At the Otto Suhr Institute, I am associated with the Center for Comparative and International Political Economy. I am also a Fellow at the <a href="https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en/home"> Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research</a>. Before joining Freie Universität as a researcher, I studied international relations and political science in Dresden and Berlin. I also worked as a Research Assistant at the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research, the <a href="https://www.rifs-potsdam.de/en"> Research Institute for Sustainability</a> in Potsdam, and the Institute of Political Science at <a href="https://tu-dresden.de/gsw/phil/powi?set_language=en"> Dresden University of Technology</a>.</p>
